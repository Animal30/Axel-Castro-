1.Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Nate Velazquez Nutrition Coaching (“Nate Velazquez,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit natevelazquez.com, interact with our Facebook or Instagram pages and ads, fill out one of our forms, book a call, or become a coaching client (together, the “Services”).

We provide 1:1 online nutrition and lifestyle coaching. We are not a medical provider, and our coaching is not a substitute for medical advice.

2.Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number, and Instagram or Facebook username.
  • Form and application answers: your goals, current habits, schedule, and anything you tell us in a lead form, application, questionnaire or message.
  • Coaching information (clients only): age, height, weight, body measurements, progress photos, food preferences, eating habits, activity level, sleep and stress, and general health information you choose to share (for example, injuries, allergies or conditions relevant to your plan).
  • Payment information: when you purchase coaching, payments are processed by our payment providers (such as Stripe or PayPal). We receive confirmation of payment and limited details (like the last four digits of a card), but we do not see or store your full card number.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, direct message, text, WhatsApp or through our coaching app, and notes from calls you book with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, links clicked, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Advertising data: information from cookies and pixels (such as the Meta Pixel) about how you interact with our website and ads. See Section 5.

Information from third parties

  • If you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form, Meta shares with us the information you entered in that form (for example, your name, email and phone number, and your answers).
  • Scheduling, payment and coaching-app providers may share with us information needed to confirm bookings, payments and your progress.

3.How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests, and contact you about the coaching you asked about.
  • Schedule and hold consultation and coaching calls.
  • Create, deliver and adjust your personalized nutrition, habit and movement plans, and track your progress.
  • Process payments, send receipts, and manage your coaching program.
  • Send you updates, tips and offers, if you have agreed to receive them (you can opt out at any time).
  • Measure and improve our website, content and advertising, including showing relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Keep our Services safe, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Health and progress information you share as a client is used only to provide your coaching. We do not use it for advertising and we do not share it with advertisers.

Testimonials: we will only publish your name, story, quotes or before-and-after photos with your explicit written permission, and you can withdraw that permission at any time.

4.Facebook, Instagram & Meta ads

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram, which are operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. (“Meta”). When you interact with our ads or submit a lead form:

  • The information you enter in a lead form is shared with us so we can contact you about the coaching you requested. We use it only for that purpose and as described in this policy.
  • Meta may collect information about your interactions with our ads under its own policies. Meta's use of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • You can control the ads you see in your Facebook Ad Preferences and Instagram settings.

5.Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to keep the site working, understand how visitors use it, and measure our ads. This may include the Meta Pixel and analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising at optout.aboutads.info. Some parts of the website may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

7.How long we keep your information

  • Leads (people who contacted us but did not become clients): up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Clients: for the length of your coaching and up to 3 years afterward, so we can support you if you return and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • Payment and tax records may be kept longer when the law requires it.

When we no longer need your information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8.How we protect your information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including password-protected accounts, limited access to client files, and trusted service providers that use encryption. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if required by law in the event of a breach.

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Access the personal information we have about you.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your personal information.
  • Stop receiving marketing emails, texts or messages.
  • Withdraw your permission for testimonials or photos.

10.Emails, texts & messages

You can opt out of marketing at any time by clicking “unsubscribe” in an email, replying STOP to a text message, or telling us in a message. Message and data rates may apply. We do not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing purposes.

11.Children's privacy

Our Services are intended for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.
