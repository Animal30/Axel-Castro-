# Nate Velazquez: brand philosophy and content rules (PRIMARY, 2026-11-21)

Source: Nate's own message to the client group chat. Treat as top priority for every new video and caption.

## Philosophy
Evidence based nutrition, sustainability, balance, a healthier relationship with food.
Key line: **flexible dieting**. You can eat whatever you want, but focus on hitting your protein goals. There is no bad food, only control and moderation.

## Never
- Fear based messaging, guilt about eating, unnecessary restrictions.
- Unsupported health or nutrition claims (e.g. "eat X at the wrong time", detoxes, "avoid X").
- Labeling foods as inherently good or bad (no good/bad/worst/avoid rankings).
- Extreme diets, detoxes, quick fixes.

## Always
- Educate, empower, help people make informed decisions. Viewers should leave more confident, not more restricted.
- Creative hooks and trending formats are fine if the information is accurate.
- Nate reviews nutrition/educational videos BEFORE they are published. Keep videos ready in the shared folder, ideally several at the start of the week.
- Nate will send an Instagram page whose style he likes. Analyze it when it arrives.

## Content pillars
1. Nutrition education and myth-busting (eating after 8 PM, carbs aren't the enemy, eating less isn't always the answer, cutting sugar, calories/protein/portions).
2. Sustainable habits and lifestyle (small habits, consistency when busy, weekends, vacations without guilt, after an off weekend).
3. Food freedom and mindset (one meal doesn't ruin progress, don't earn food through exercise, good/bad labels are harmful, consistent vs restrictive).
4. Body recomposition and practical nutrition (balanced high-protein meal, fat loss vs weight loss, easy high-protein meals, strength training + protein, grocery shopping on a budget).
5. Real-life coaching and personal connection (what I wish people understood, 3 things I'd never tell a client, why no extreme diets, what sustainable progress looks like, handling setbacks).

## Captions
Same best-performing format (hook, 2 lines in Nate's voice, ONE CTA: DM "PROTEIN", 3 hashtags, pinned first-comment question), without fear or good/bad language.
