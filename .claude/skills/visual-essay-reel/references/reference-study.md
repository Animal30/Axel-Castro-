# Style study: the evidence-wall visual essay (a 54 s, 720x1280 reference reel)

This is what the reference reel DOES, written as a description of the style. The skill recreates the system
(hierarchy, pacing, evidence, hard cuts), not the frames, and swaps the reference's gradients for black with a light in
the creator's accent colour. It is the baseline the creator's own references are compared against at setup.

## What is on screen
- **Presenter**: cut out of their room, lower half of the frame. Top of the head 45-50% down, head about 16% of the
  frame height, shoulders run off the bottom. They are in front of everything.
- **Backdrop**: a grainy vertical gradient (dark at the top, hot at the bottom). The hue shifts between chapters of the
  script, always on a cut.
- **Evidence**: about 25 visuals in 54 s, one every ~2 s, each on the phrase that names it:

  | The presenter says | We see |
  |---|---|
  | "the ads for this app" | screen recording of the app's ad, on a white mat |
  | "and they're likely right" | the ad's own citations panel (studies, journals) |
  | "brands are hiring armies of..." | the meme graphic, then the actual tweet, then the article card |
  | "into the replies ... sales call" | photo of the billboard filling the lower half, the presenter moved up and to one side; a second card laid over it |
  | "it's become a meme / it works" | tweet; a reply tweet laid over it on "it works" |
  | a person who is named | that person's own thumbnail or photo |
  | a funding announcement | the announcement image |
  | "A versus B" | two cut-out heads appearing one after the other, side by side |
  | closing line | the opening collage again (bookend) |

- **Card treatment**: evidence keeps its own look (a tweet is a white tweet, square-ish corners). Width about 88% of the
  frame, top edge 5-8% down, the lower edge tucks behind the presenter's head. Screen recordings and photos sit on a
  white mat. One card at a time, except overlays (a reply over a tweet) and deliberate pairs.
- **Full-bleed**: opening and closing collage of faces covering the whole frame, tinted, the presenter smaller in the
  middle with the title over the chest; a photo filling the top 60% and melting into the gradient; a tinted silhouette of
  a person standing behind them.
- **Title**: bold serif line + smaller sans line, white, over the chest, first 2-3 s only.
- **Captions**: small white bold sans, 1-3 words, thin dark edge, ONE fixed height at about 45% (just above the head,
  over the bottom of the card). No emphasis styling, no stacks.

## How it moves
- **Hard cuts.** Evidence appears and is replaced on a single frame; nothing slides, spins or bounces.
- The presenter's size and position change only on those cuts (bigger and to one side for a tall card, small in a corner
  for a full-bleed photo), which also hides jump cuts.
- Pairs build one item at a time on the word.
- Energy comes from the information changing, not from things moving.

## What this skill changes on purpose
Black backdrop lit in the brand accent colour instead of the gradients · a 0.18 s scale-in and a slow 3% push on cards (a
hard cut is still available with how="cut") · captions in the brand's caption font with the emphasis font in the accent
colour (the reference has no emphasis) · readable content kept inside the platform safe zone (the reference runs cards to
the very top) · a soft shadow under cards so they sit in the black.

The reference stills (layout sheets, a hard-cut strip) are not shipped with the template: they show a third party's
frames. Build the creator's own reference study at setup instead (see `setup/SETUP.md`, "References they love").
