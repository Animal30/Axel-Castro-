## Cláusulas corregidas (en inglés, para pegar en el contrato)

### Section 7 — ISBN

> **7. ISBN.** The Client may choose one of the following options for each format of the Work (e.g., ebook, paperback, hardcover):
>
> **(a) Free KDP ISBN.** If the Client selects a free ISBN provided by Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing ("KDP"), the ISBN is assigned and registered by KDP, the imprint will appear as "Independently published," and the ISBN may be used only for distribution through KDP. The Client remains the author and copyright owner of the Work.
>
> **(b) Client-Purchased ISBN.** If the Client purchases ISBNs (e.g., from Bowker, the official ISBN agency for the United States and its territories), the ISBNs will be registered in the Client's name as publisher and may carry the Client's own imprint name. The Provider will assist with entering the ISBN and imprint information but does not purchase ISBNs on the Client's behalf unless agreed in writing.
>
> Under either option, the KDP account used to publish the Work will be owned by and registered to the Client.

### Sections 4.2 and 4.3 — Ownership and files

> **4.2 Ownership of Deliverables.** Upon receipt of full payment, the Provider assigns to the Client all right, title and interest, including copyright, in the final deliverables created by the Provider under this Agreement, including the cover design, interior layout, and any original artwork. The Provider will deliver to the Client: (i) the final print-ready and ebook files, and (ii) the editable source files `[e.g., .indd / .psd / .ai / Canva link / .docx]`. The Provider may display the finished work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.
>
> **4.3 Third-Party Assets.** Where the deliverables include fonts, stock images or other design assets owned by third parties ("Third-Party Assets"), the Provider will use only assets whose licenses permit perpetual, worldwide commercial use in the Work, including in all editions, formats and marketing materials for the Work, and such use will continue after this Agreement ends. The Provider will give the Client a list of all Third-Party Assets used and their license terms. Where a license cannot be transferred to the Client (for example, font software licensed to a single user), the Client's right to use the finished Work is not affected; however, if the Client wishes to edit the source files, the Client may need to obtain their own license for that asset, or the Provider will substitute a freely licensed alternative at the Client's request.

### Section 13 — Early termination

> **13. Termination and Refunds.** Either party may terminate this Agreement with written notice. On termination, the value of work completed is calculated according to the following milestones:
