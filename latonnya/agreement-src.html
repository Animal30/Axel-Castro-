This Agreement describes how Maison Enterprises (“Maison”) will take the Client's book, “I'm 93% Christian! Don't Judge Me!” (the “Work”), from finished manuscript to a professionally published, translated, and marketed book. The Client stays the full owner of her book at every step. Maison's role is to handle the entire process for her: formatting, design, translation, publication, and marketing.

The key terms, in plain language

  • You are the full owner of your book, in every language and format.
  • You keep 100% of your royalties, paid by Amazon directly to you.
  • Your Amazon, Meta, and website accounts are all in your name.
  • The fee below is a one-time production fee. Maison's work is the service itself.
  • You approve every cover, translation, and ad before it goes live.
  • Every cover, translation, website, and video made for you is yours.
  • Advertising starts after publication, with a small test budget that grows with results.
  • You set the pace of the project and can end it at any time (Section 13).

01Services and Your Role as Author

Maison will provide the services described in Schedule A under the All Inclusive plan (the “Services”). This is a self-publishing service: the Client is the author, owner, and publisher of her own Work in every edition, and Maison works on her behalf. Additional services can be added at any time with a written quote agreed by both parties.

02How the Process Works

The project runs in four stages. The Client's investment goes entirely into production first. Paid advertising begins only once the book is published.

STAGE 1
Onboarding
The Client shares the final manuscript and any photos or material she would like to use. Maison sets up the Amazon KDP account, Meta account, and domain in her name, together with her.
STAGE 2
Production + Organic Marketing
Maison formats the interior, designs the covers, and prepares the translations. At the same time, organic content starts building an audience for the book, with no ad spend.
STAGE 3
Publication
After the Client's approval, the English edition goes live on Amazon, followed by the Spanish, Portuguese, and French editions, and the author website.
STAGE 4
Launch + Paid Advertising
With the book live, Maison launches the Meta Ads campaign with a small test budget, then scales it step by step based on real results (Section 9).

Communication. Throughout the project, the Client has a direct line with Maison by email and message, receives a progress update every week, and gets a reply within one business day.

04Copyright and Ownership

  • 4.1The Work. The Client is and remains the sole owner of all copyright and other rights in the Work, including the manuscript, its title, and its content, in every language and format. All rights in the Work stay with the Client at all times.
  • 4.2Everything created for the Client. All materials Maison creates for the Client under this Agreement (the “Deliverables”), including the covers, interior files, translations, website content and files, videos, ad creatives, and press kit, belong to the Client. Upon payment of the fee, Maison transfers to the Client all rights in the Deliverables. The translations are editions of the Client's own Work and belong to her. The Client owns the finished Deliverables and receives both the final files (including the print-ready and ebook files, cover files, website files, and videos) and the editable source files for each of them.
  • 4.3Design assets. Maison uses only images, fonts, and design elements whose licenses allow commercial use on a permanent basis. The Client has a permanent, royalty-free right to use them as part of her book (in every edition, language, and format), her website, and her marketing, and this right continues after this Agreement ends. Maison gives the Client a list of every third-party asset used, with its source and license. Some font licenses cannot be transferred from one person to another; this never limits the Client's use of the finished files. If the Client later wants to edit the source files herself and a font license does not cover her, Maison will replace that font with a freely licensed one at no cost, or she may purchase her own license.
  • 4.4Portfolio. Once the book is published, Maison may show the published covers and website in its portfolio. The manuscript itself stays private. The Client may ask Maison to remove these samples at any time.

05Permission to Work on the Book

So that Maison can do its job, the Client gives Maison a limited, non-exclusive permission to use, format, translate, and adapt the Work only to perform the Services and to publish it under the Client's own accounts. This permission ends when the Services are complete. It gives Maison no publishing, print, ebook, audio, film, or territorial rights of any kind.

06Amazon KDP Account and Royalties

Every edition will be published through an Amazon KDP account registered in the Client's name, with her own banking and tax information. 100% of the royalties from every sale, in every language and format, are paid by Amazon directly to the Client. Maison's compensation is the one-time fee in Section 3, and nothing else.

The Client will give Maison access to the KDP account in whatever way she prefers, so Maison can upload and manage the listings. Maison works only on the books and listings. The Client's banking, tax, and payment details stay private to her, and she can update her password or access at any time.

07ISBNs

For each edition, the Client chooses the ISBN option she prefers:

  • Free KDP ISBN: assigned and registered by Amazon KDP at no cost, ideal for books sold on Amazon. The listing shows “Independently published” as the publisher, and this ISBN can be used only for that edition on Amazon KDP.
  • Her own ISBN: purchased by the Client from Bowker (currently about $125 USD each, or less in blocks of 10) and registered in her name. Her name, or an imprint name she chooses, appears as the publisher, and the ISBN works on any platform.

Under either option, the Client remains the author and sole owner of the Work, and every edition is published from her own KDP account. Maison will guide the Client through either option at no extra cost.

08Author Website and Domain

The author website's domain is registered in the Client's name. Maison covers the domain and hosting for the first 12 months. From the second year, the Client simply renews the domain directly (currently about $15 to $20 USD per year), with no maintenance fee from Maison. Whenever the Client wishes, Maison will hand over the domain, the hosting, and a complete copy of the website to her own accounts.

09Marketing and Advertising

During production: while the book is being formatted, designed, and translated, Maison works on organic marketing, building interest in the book through content, with no advertising budget needed.

After publication: once the book is live on Amazon, Maison launches the Meta Ads campaign. The approach is to test first with a low budget, learn what works, and then scale with confidence:

STEP 1 · TEST
$100 to $200
Starting budget for the first month after publication, to test audiences and ads.
STEP 2 · OPTIMIZE
Best ads
Maison keeps the ads and audiences that sell the book and refines the rest.
STEP 3 · SCALE
Grows with results
The budget increases gradually, always with the Client's approval.

The campaign runs from a Meta ad account owned by the Client, with Maison added as a partner. The Client pays Meta directly and sees every result in real time. Campaign management is included for 60 days from publication, and can be extended afterward if the Client wishes.

10Your Approval at Every Step

The Client has final approval over every cover, the interior layout, every translation, the website, every video, and every ad. Everything goes live on Amazon, the website, or Meta only after her written approval (an email or message is enough). The project timeline follows the Client's review pace.

11Translations

Each translation (Spanish, Portuguese, and French) is a complete translation of the Work, reviewed in full by a fluent human translator to preserve the Client's voice, humor, and faith language. Translations are delivered first as manuscript files, so the Client, or any reader she trusts, can review them. Corrections are included until she is fully happy and gives her approval.

12Materials

The Client provides the manuscript and the materials she owns (such as her original illustrated cover and personal photos). Maison provides everything it creates and every design asset it uses. Each party stands behind the materials it provides.

13Flexibility

Either party may end this Agreement with written notice. In that case, Maison delivers to the Client every file completed so far, and any amount paid beyond the work already completed is refunded. The Client keeps her book, her accounts, and every deliverable she has paid for.

14Confidentiality

The manuscript, the translations, and any personal information the Client shares are kept strictly confidential. They are shared only with the Maison team members working on the book, and used only for the Services.

15Our Commitment

Maison commits to delivering every Service with professional quality, clear communication, and full transparency. Marketing decisions are based on real data, and results are shared openly with the Client. If Amazon or Meta request any adjustment to a file, listing, or ad, Maison takes care of it at no extra cost.

16General Terms

Maison works as an independent service provider. This Agreement, together with Schedule A, is the complete agreement between the parties. Any change is agreed in writing by both parties. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico, where the Client resides, and applicable United States federal law. If a disagreement arises, the parties will first try in good faith to resolve it by direct conversation and, if needed, through mediation conducted online. If it is not resolved within 30 days, it may be submitted to the competent courts of Puerto Rico. This Agreement may be signed electronically.
